# Initial design plans

A · Ranked list. Ink #101820, flat slate #151F28, chalk #F1F3EE, mint #96DFC4 and caveat amber #FFC676. Space Grotesk throughout, with a same-layout Barlow Semi Condensed / Instrument Sans trial. Reading order is left to right from rank to champion to aligned evidence.

    Home   [promise + actual sample ranks] [queue + context →]
    Picks  [role navigation and ordering                   ]
           [rank | face | name | scored evidence] [ban rail]
    Phone  [role navigation] [draft jump] [#1] [#2] [#3] …

B · Pick brief. Cool white #F4F5F9, paper #FFFFFF, pale lilac #EFEDF9, ink #21213B and iris #5142AC. Barlow Semi Condensed for the wordmark and headlines, Instrument Sans for controls and evidence. The comparison bracket anchors open decisions and frames two valid intents, pick and deny.

    Home   [promise] [one guided queue and context surface  ]
    Picks  [Picks | Draft] [role navigation]
           [portrait | recommendation and why | evidence   ]
           [alternatives you can inspect in the same brief ]
    Draft  [role-specific denial list] [five-role roster   ]

Review before build. Both tool suggestions came from generic showcase templates. A keeps the useful density dial and replaces the sample dashboard motif with a real ranked sequence. Home previews actual champions and links its context to the upcoming setup. B uses contrast and whitespace from the minimalism result, but trades the conventional headline/feature/CTA sequence for one setup instrument and a replaceable evidence brief. Role selection and labeled evidence stay immediate. No motion or decorative hero art.
